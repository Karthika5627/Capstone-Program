Problem Statement for Medical Lab Test Booking & Report Portal:
       
    .The traditional process of booking medical laboratory tests and collecting test reports is often time-consuming and inconvenient. 
    .Patients usually need to visit the laboratory in person to schedule appointments, wait in long queues, and return again to collect 
     their reports.
    .This manual process can lead to appointment conflicts, delays, misplaced records, and poor communication between patients and 
     laboratories. Additionally, there is no centralized system for securely storing and accessing previous test reports.
    .The Medical Lab Test Booking & Report Portal aims to solve these issues by providing an online platform where patients can register, 
     book lab tests, manage appointments, make payments (optional), and securely access their test reports from anywhere.
